Since Souq Waqif

A Table Between Three Worlds

Desert Rose brings Arabic, Ukrainian and European kitchens together under one roof at Al Jomrok Hotel — a rooftop terrace overlooking the lanes of Souq Waqif, where the call to prayer, sizzling grills and the city skyline all meet.

By day it's a quiet corner for coffee and shisha; by night, the terrace fills with lantern light, live buzz and the smell of char-grilled kebab drifting over old Doha.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a reservation before visiting?

Reservations are recommended, especially for dinner and weekends. You can book directly through our Booking page, by phone, or via WhatsApp.

What type of cuisine do you serve?

We serve Arabic, Ukrainian and European dishes — mezze, grills, borscht and continental mains — all on one menu.

Is shisha available?

Yes, we offer a wide selection of quality shisha flavors on the terrace, alongside our full food and beverage menu.

Where exactly are you located?

We're at Al Jomrok Hotel, Souq Waqif, Doha — right at the entrance as you come up from the underground parking.
