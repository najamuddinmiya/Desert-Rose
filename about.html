Our Story

Three Kitchens, One Table

Where Doha Meets the Wider World

Desert Rose opened its doors on the terrace of Al Jomrok Hotel, right at the edge of Souq Waqif, with a simple idea: bring Arabic, Ukrainian and European kitchens together under one roof, without either losing its character.

Our chefs char-grill kebabs over open flame the traditional way, simmer borscht low and slow, and finish European mains with the same care you'd expect on a European high street — all served a few steps from the oldest market lanes in the city.

Day and Night

A Terrace That Changes With the Hours

By afternoon, Desert Rose is a quiet corner for coffee and shisha overlooking the souq. As evening falls, lanterns come on, the grill smoke rises, and the terrace fills with the sound of the market below and the skyline lighting up beyond it.
